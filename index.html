HOW TO PLAY

🕹️

Move

Drag anywhere on the left side. Push the stick all the way to sprint. Keyboard: WASD, Shift to sprint.

⚽

Shoot

Tap SHOOT for a quick shot. Hold it to charge a power shot, then let go. Keyboard: K or Space.

➡️

Pass

PASS sends the ball to the teammate you are facing. Without the ball it becomes PASS ME: your teammates will pass to you. Keyboard: J.

💥

Steal

Without the ball, the big button becomes STEAL. Run at the player with the ball and tap it to grab the ball.

RIFT PASS

1

Play good football to fill your Rift Energy: passes, interceptions, tackles, saves and shots on target.

2

Tap RIFT. Time slows down.

3

Tap the pitch to place the entry rift near you.

4

Tap again to place the exit rift. Drag while placing to aim it; just tap and it aims at the goal.

5

The dotted line shows exactly where the ball will go. Press PASS to send it through.

Rifts only move the ball, never players. Anyone can send the ball through them, opponents too. They close a moment after the ball goes through, or after 8 seconds.
